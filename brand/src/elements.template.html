The floor only rises

Wavefront launches coins that own their own liquidity. Every trade pays into a floor that only rises, and holders can borrow that floor without selling.

Coin and wallet glyphs

Every coin and wallet gets a picture, even with none uploaded: a pattern of blocks worked out from its address, always the same for the same address. Squares for coins, discs for wallets. glyph(seed)

Icons

Thirteen icons on a 9 by 9 block grid. They take the colour of the text around them. icon(name), or the files in elements/icons/.

Numbers in blocks

Where a real chart would be noise: a price line and a share, counted in blocks. The coins and prices on these cards are made up. spark(points) and meter(value)

Buttons, chips and a loader

One filled button a screen, in Tide. Chips are small mono labels. The loader passes a wave along five blocks. All in elements/wavefront.css.

Fluid

How the market is drawn: a Foam line with bands of water under it. On its own it sits behind headers and at the foot of a page. Over a floor, it stops a little short of the blocks.

Dividers and pattern

Between major sections, a rule of blocks that comes loose at its end. Behind headers and on cards for sharing, the sea over the floor.
