The floor

Blocks: the quote under the floor price, across the coins held. Every coin bought was paid for at the floor or more, and this is the part that guarantees it.

The market

Fluid: what buyers paid above the floor, up to the curve. Together with the floor it is all the quote in the pool.

The floor ahead

One row of empty blocks, out to the end of the supply: coins nobody has bought, so there is nothing under them yet. The faint line is where the price goes as they are bought.

Lent out

A loan takes the floor under the coins borrowed against: those coins times the floor price. The coins are locked and the market above is untouched, so there is nothing to liquidate.

Drawn from the protocol's own formulas (see docs/bonding-curve.md): with k as virtual quote times supply, the price when a number of coins are held is k ÷ (supply − held)². It starts at the floor, and the area under it up to the coins held is the quote in the pool. Fees are 2% a trade, with 60% healed on a buy or burned on a sell. On Uniswap v4 a heal or burn waits until it is worth one tick step; here it lands at once, as it does in v1.
