How Wavefront looks. It is drawn in the block language it shares with GlazeCorp: rounded blocks, a wave made of them, and a dark ground lit in afterglow colours: blue, violet, pink and amber.

The mark

The Tide: a coin made of blocks, filled to a wave line.

What it shows

The disc is a coin. The coloured blocks are what backs it, shading from blue at the bottom through violet to pink. The amber line on top is the wave: the edge where that backing is rising. The empty blocks above are room still to fill.

How it is built

A 9 by 9 grid, corners dropped to make a disc. Every block leaves a gap of 14% of its cell and rounds its corners by 22% of its own width, the same numbers as every other block in the system.

The wave line, the fill level and the order of the colours are fixed in the logo. Don't redraw them.

Three sizes

Each smaller size is redrawn on a coarser grid, so the blocks never get too fine to read.

Logo

The mark with the name in Funnel Display Medium. The gap is 28% of the mark's height and the type is 86% of it.

On dark

The default. The mark sits straight on the ground with no disc.

Wordmark

On its own only where the mark is already nearby.

Keep clear space of a quarter of the mark's height on every side. The smallest lockup is 20px tall; below that, use the mark alone.

Colour

A near-black ground, ash type and the Afterglow ramp: five colours, sea floor to foam. Tide, the violet, is the accent.

Ground and type

Meaning

Only for what they say: price up, price down, caution. Green is never a brand colour here.

On the dark ground Tide reads at :1 and Foam at :1, so both work for text. On white, Tide is only :1; use the darker violet there (:1). Text on a Tide button is the ground colour (:1). The ramp always runs in the same order, Trench up to Foam; don't shuffle it.

Blocks

Anything beyond the logo is built from the same block: patterns, dividers, meters. A shape made of blocks may come loose at its leading edge, and nowhere else.

Swell

The background pattern. The wave line is always Foam and there is always water under it.

Meter

A share counted in whole blocks. The leading block is Foam.

Rule

Between major sections of a page. Never inside a form.

The front

Wavefront draws in two ways, and its signature is the place where they meet. Blocks are for things you count: the logo, coins, icons, meters. Fluid, in smooth layers, is for things that flow: a price curve, a background. At a front, fluid sets into blocks and blocks melt back into fluid. That is what a trade does at the price, with quote on one side and coins on the other.

How fluid is built

A line in Foam, with water under it in section: Surf, Tide, Deep, then Trench. Each band follows the line and is about twice as thick as the one above it. Flat colour only: no gradients, no glow.

How they meet

The fluid's edge is bitten into block-shaped notches, more of them toward the front. Past the front, loose blocks gather into solid rows. Nothing fades: every part is fluid, a block, or empty.

The rule

Fluid and blocks meet side by side, at a front. Never draw a line or a fluid shape on top of blocks. The logo is always blocks: it is the coin, and coins are the side that has set.

Mechanics

Pictures of the protocol are drawn to scale from its own formulas, never sketched. Price runs left to right, depth runs up, and one block is a fixed amount of quote, so each action is a change of shape you can count. Each card shows before on top and after underneath.

Buy

Quote fills in behind the front as it moves up the price.

Sell

The front falls back. The floor is its left wall.

Heal

More quote, nothing taken out: taller, and the wall moves in.

Burn

Fewer coins: thinner, with both the floor and the price further right.

Borrow

A loan takes the quote sitting at the floor, against locked coins. The floor price stays put.

In motion

graphics/liquidity.html is the same picture animated: it plays through trades, heals, burns and loans, and has buttons to try each one. When liquidity moves it sloshes and settles; when the pool is rebuilt, the blocks lift out and drop back in.

graphics/floor.html is the floor through time: a price line over a floor that climbs in steps and never comes back down.

Site elements

The pieces a site is built from live in elements/, with a gallery of all of them in elements.html: a living hero of block water, glyphs that give every coin and wallet a picture, thirteen block icons, price lines and meters in blocks, buttons, chips and a loader.

Don't

  • Recolour the mark or reorder its colours. It is the Afterglow ramp on the dark ground, or one flat colour.
  • Use the full mark below 48px. Switch to Small, then Micro.
  • Put the disc-less mark on a light ground, or on anything lighter than the surface colour.
  • Change the wave line, the fill level or the block spacing.
  • Stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows and glows.
  • Draw a line or a fluid shape over blocks. The two meet side by side at a front, or not at all.
  • Set the name in another face, or type it as "WaveFront" or "Wave Front". It is one word: Wavefront.
