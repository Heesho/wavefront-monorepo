The floor only rises

Every trade pays a little into the floor. The price can fall back to it, and never through it.

Drifting

This runs live on the protocol's own formulas: a 2% fee on every trade, with 60% of it healed into the curve on a buy and burned on a sell. Nothing in the picture tells the floor to go up. Press Dump to watch the price fall onto the floor and stop.

Each column is fourteen trades on a very busy coin, about changing hands, so years of ordinary trading pass in a minute. The scale is logarithmic: a doubling is the same height anywhere.
